About the Developer

Built by a cybersecurity student passionate about privacy tools. With a background in computer science and a focus on cryptography, CryptBox was created to make strong encryption accessible to everyone while maintaining the highest security standards.

The project began as a personal tool to securely share files with colleagues, but quickly evolved into a full-featured application designed to protect sensitive information in an increasingly data-driven world.
